Our story and our team

About Coffee Shop

A small café concept built around warm hospitality, useful study space and coffee without fuss.

A simple idea, made welcoming

Coffee Shop began as a student project by DMT, a two-person team from group SE-2509. We wanted to create the kind of neighbourhood café we would use ourselves: calm, affordable and easy to settle into.

Our short menu keeps the focus on well-made classics and fresh snacks. Whether you arrive with a laptop or a friend, there is always time for a proper coffee break.
